About CampusConnect

CampusConnect is a college event management platform built to streamline the discovery, registration, and administration of campus events.

Key Capabilities
  • Event Discovery: Students can explore workshops, seminars, competitions, and college fests.
  • Seat Reservation: Real-time seat allocation with ACID transactions.
  • Admin Control: Event lifecycle control, registration status updates, and student records.
